What this can — and can't — do

Can Block known AI sites & search ›

Type or paste a link and AIBlockle warns you before you open a known AI service (ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Midjourney and dozens more). Search runs through DuckDuckGo, which shows plain results with no AI answer panel.

Can't Filter AI inside other websites ›

A web page can't reach inside another website to hide its AI images or text — phone browser security blocks that for everyone. Doing that needs a browser extension (which phone Chrome doesn't allow) or a filtering proxy. This page guards the front door; it can't edit other pages once you're on them.

Can't Detect every AI perfectly ›

AI-written text and AI images often look identical to real ones, and new AI tools appear constantly. AIBlockle catches what's on its list of known AI services — not every possible one. It's honest about that on purpose.
